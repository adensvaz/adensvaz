# Aden Savio Vaz

**Senior Product Manager · AI & LLM Products · Regulated Fintech, Payments and Digital Assets · Dubai, UAE**

Aden Savio Vaz is a Senior Product Manager based in Dubai who builds and ships AI products inside regulated financial environments. He has twelve years of experience across crypto exchanges, payments infrastructure, custody, stablecoin issuance, CBDC and compliance, and he publishes the evaluation behind what he ships, not just the demo.

### What I work on

**AI & LLM Products.** Agentic systems, RAG pipelines, LLM-driven signal generation, document extraction with provenance, AI-assisted underwriting, and consumer AI assistants. I care about the part most demos skip: how you know the model is actually working.

**Regulated Product Delivery.** KYC, AML and on-chain screening, sanctions screening, functional specification authoring, and vendor governance through to in-house ownership.

### How I think about AI products

Most AI demos work once. The interesting question is what happens on the tenth run, at scale, with real users and real money.

So the things I build carry an evaluation layer: a defined pre-launch validation phase, explicit metrics, benchmarking against the previous iteration, documented failure cases, and monitoring after launch on the same metrics used before it. In regulated finance you cannot ship a black box, and that constraint usually makes the product better.
