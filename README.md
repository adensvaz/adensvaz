### What I work on

**AI & LLM Products.** Agentic systems, RAG pipelines, LLM-driven signal generation, document extraction with provenance, rules-to-timeline engines, and AI-assisted underwriting. I care about the part most demos skip: how you know the model is actually working.

**Model Evaluation & Monitoring.** Paper-trading validation, out-of-sample and walk-forward testing, iteration-over-iteration benchmarking on risk, maximum drawdown and P&L, and post-launch monitoring on the same metrics used before launch.

**Payments & Settlement.** Order management systems, liquidity routing, market making, fiat on and off ramps, payment gateway and PSP integration, atomic settlement, CBDC and stablecoin infrastructure.

**Digital Asset Infrastructure.** Centralised and decentralised exchanges, custody and wallets, staking and DeFi yield, tokenomics, Layer-3 and EVM chains, node and validator operations.

**Permissioned & Consortium Blockchain.** Hyperledger and Corda style permissioned ledgers, consortium governance, validator onboarding, smart contracts, enterprise blockchain delivery for Vodafone, Nokia and Huawei.

**Regulated Product Delivery.** KYC, AML and on-chain screening, sanctions screening, sourced-and-dated rule systems, functional specification authoring, and vendor governance through to in-house ownership.
